# Start Here: One Delegated Action in Thirty Minutes

Choose one consequential action you would like AI to assist or perform. Do not
map “the agent” as one permission.

## 0–5 minutes: name the useful work

Complete three sentences:

1. A person or team currently spends time ___;
2. AI assistance could help by ___;
3. but the neighboring power that must remain withheld is ___.

If no affected person, protected object, or consequence can be named, the
boundary is still too abstract.

## 5–15 minutes: map one authority

Complete the [six-line first map](agent-authority-map.md#ten-minute-first-map)
in the Agent Authority Map. Compare it with the
[miniature Northbridge example](agent-authority-map.md#miniature-example).

Stop if tool access is being used as evidence of legitimate authority.

## 15–25 minutes: follow the action path

Use the [Governed Tool Contract](governed-tool-contract.md) to identify the
enforcement point, allowed operation, preconditions, outcomes, retries,
evidence, and revocation behavior. Add one limit from the
[Action Budget and Blast-Radius Worksheet](action-budget-and-blast-radius.md).

## 25–30 minutes: ask for a read-back

Give the completed action row to someone outside the agent team. Ask what the
agent may do, what it may not do, who granted the power, where it is enforced,
and what stops or reopens the decision.

## Your first result

You now have a reviewable delegation hypothesis, not an approved autonomous
system. Continue with the [Northbridge worked examples](NORTHBRIDGE-WORKED-EXAMPLES.md)
and the [unrelated transfer scenario](TRANSFER-SCENARIO-WORKED-EXAMPLES.md),
then add memory, evidence-gate, and incident obligations proportionate to the
consequence.
