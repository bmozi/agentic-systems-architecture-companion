# Repository Instructions

- This repository is the reader-facing companion to one standalone field guide.
- The book must remain independently useful; do not reproduce manuscript prose
  or make the book depend on this repository.
- Lead with the reader's recognizable need and one useful first result before
  specialist terminology.
- Preserve constructed, scenario, planned, unrun, observed, tested, reported,
  inferred, and unknown evidence states. Do not turn a template into a control
  claim or a completed example into production evidence.
- Do not invent John Briggs's lived experience. Use genuine, anonymized, or
  transparently constructed material with provenance.
- Keep licensing, usability, security, edition, and publication states explicit.
- Run `python3 scripts/validate_repository.py` before committing.
