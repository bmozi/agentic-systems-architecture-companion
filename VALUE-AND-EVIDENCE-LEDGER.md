# Value and Evidence Ledger

Use this to connect an agentic capability to useful work and measured evidence
without treating conversation quality as business value.

## Five-minute first row

| Delegated work | Who benefits | Current friction | Bounded agent role | Evidence to collect | Decision it informs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Draft and route a partner-credit recommendation | Service specialist and partner | Manual policy search and routing; approval remains human | Retrieve cited policy, prepare recommendation, invoke governed routing tool | Review time, unsupported recommendation rate, abstention, correction, tool receipts, downstream completion | Keep assistive, revise, or permit a narrower approved action |

## Working ledger

| Delegated work | Beneficiary | Baseline/source | Allowed action | Withheld power | Leading evidence | Outcome evidence | Incidents/near misses | Cost/tradeoff | Owner | Expansion or stop trigger |

## Evidence rules

- Label values **observed**, **reported**, **estimated**, or **unknown**.
- Measure work and consequences, not fluency, confidence, or demo appeal.
- Count human review, abstention, correction, rejected actions, tool effects,
  downstream events, and open workflow obligations.
- Preserve incidents, near misses, and boundary failures with successes.
- Do not infer productivity, safety, or return on investment from offline model
  evaluation alone.

## Monday-morning use

Choose one repetitive decision or action with a named owner and reversible
consequence. Measure the current work before adding an agent. A useful pilot may
show that advising is valuable while acting should remain withheld.
