## By role

- **Agent developer:** begin with one action, one governed tool, explicit state,
  and receipts—not one permission for the whole agent.
- **Architect:** connect APIs, MCP tools, events, workflows, memory, people, and
  evidence while preserving each boundary.
- **Security or governance:** review principal, subject, purpose, protected
  object, delegation chain, enforcement, revocation, and blast radius.
- **Operations:** review durable ownership, stop paths, containment,
  reconstruction, correction, and re-entry.
- **Product or business owner:** decide which work is useful to delegate and
  which consequence remains a human or institutional decision.

Use [Role-Based Paths](ROLE-BASED-PATHS.md) for developer, architect, manager,
and executive routes with a concrete handoff artifact.
