## Scoring scale

- **2 — clear and traceable:** The response records the bounded decision,
  source/version, owner or owner gap, authority, consequence, evidence limit,
  and action in a selected record or a stable workbook disposition.
- **1 — partial or ambiguous:** The condition appears, but scope, authority,
  evidence, consequence, owner, trace, or limitation is incomplete.
- **0 — absent or unsafe:** The condition is omitted, contradicted, invented,
  or converted into unsupported certainty or authority.
- **N/A — not scoreable:** Evidence is unavailable because of a recorded stop
  or packet defect. Explain; do not replace N/A with zero.

Do not total unlike dimensions into a universal percentage. Use dimension
results to identify exact source revisions and retest decisions. The
facilitator guide's five-asset route is a reference route, not a compulsory
answer. A smaller route can receive full scores when every relevant decision,
owner, evidence link, limit, and separate disposition remains traceable.

## Observation prompts

Record behavior without coaching:

- first asset opened and participant-stated reason, if any;
- routing changes and the evidence that prompted them;
- skipped, duplicated, or implementation-only fields;
- terms or state labels interpreted differently;
- attempts to turn technical access, confidence, or a record status into
  authority;
- treatment of unknown tool outcome, stale memory, shared budgets, and reset;
- requests for help and whether the answer existed in supplied text;
- safe refusal, unsupported certainty, or paperwork-only completion;
- time spent locating, interpreting, deciding, and recording; and
- places where an asset could not express the decision without a workaround.
