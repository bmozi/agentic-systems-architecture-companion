## Predetermined reference route and alternative-route rule

The reference route uses **five assets**, not all six:

1. **Agent Authority Map** — separate the currently permitted read/draft path
   from the proposed tentative hold and explicitly withheld confirmation, fee,
   access, external-message, override, and onward-delegation actions. Preserve
   the absent accepted delegation source, owner, interval, revocation rule, and
   authority receipt.
2. **Memory and Provenance Record** — classify `MEM-GAC-118` as a derived item
   from policy v4, distinguish it from authoritative policy v5, expose the
   omitted descendants and missing use receipt, and prevent stale influence on
   the hold decision until correction and revalidation are governed.
3. **Action Budget and Blast-Radius Worksheet** — bind attempts and effects to
   `GAC-FACT-204` and `GAC-CASE-204`, calculate the twelve pre-control attempt
   opportunities, apply shared cross-worker boundaries, and preserve ambiguous
   counter, breaker, revocation, in-flight, and correction behavior as unrun.
4. **Autonomy Evidence Gate** — record a bounded `RESTRICTED` disposition for
   the proposed hold expansion because authority is undecided, correction and
   action-path checks are unrun, capacity is unknown, incident evidence is
   absent, and the separate release gate is already restricted.
5. **Agentic Incident Readiness Plan** — reject `GAC-RESET-017` as sufficient
   re-entry evidence; define containment beyond stopping inference, reconcile
   the accepted/unknown operation, preserve and reconstruct descendants,
   correct memory and effects, protect unaffected scope, and require a new
   bounded gate before re-entry.

The **Governed Tool Contract is not part of the reference route** because the
scenario supplies `GTC-GAC-HOLD-004` version 1.3.0 for the exact proposed call,
including original-principal propagation, external enforcement, request/egress
limits, business-operation identity, outcome meanings, ambiguous reconciliation,
and revocation targets. The participant must inspect and reference that record.
Completing another contract is unnecessary unless the participant identifies a
specific independent gap or proposed change and records why the existing
scenario record cannot answer it.

The five-asset route is not a uniquely correct answer. A participant may use
fewer assets and receive full scores when the retained outputs still:

- distinguish authenticated reachability from delegated business authority;
- preserve every allowed, proposed, and withheld action plus the original
  principal, object, purpose, time, PDP, PEP, receipts, and revocation gaps;
- trace the tool attempt through technical response, reconciliation, and
  durable business outcome without treating `Accepted` as `Completed`;
- prevent policy-v4 derived memory from silently outranking policy v5 and keep
  known and undiscovered descendant limits visible;
- bind the twelve candidate attempt opportunities to shared case-wide budgets,
  causal identity, breaker, kill, in-flight, and correction rules;
- make an evidence-bounded release disposition without promoting unrun records
  or a `REVIEWED` contract into runtime proof;
- reject reset-and-restart as incident recovery when accepted/unknown work,
  caches, queues, descendants, corrections, and re-entry authority remain; and
- explain why each skipped template adds no independent owner, evidence record,
  decision, or reconsideration trigger needed for this task.

Score decision coverage, traceability, safe exclusion, and overlap reasoning.
Do not penalize a smaller route solely because it differs from the reference
route. Do not reward five or six copied templates when the response loses a
decision boundary or substitutes form completion for judgment.

## Predetermined decision boundaries

### Authority and action

- The service identity's technical access is not evidence that
  `GAC-ROLE-SCHEDULING` delegated the proposed hold.
- Read scoped availability and draft a schedule are the only currently allowed
  agent behaviors supplied by the scenario.
- The tentative hold remains proposed and cannot be approved by this exercise
  while its accountable authority source, delegation chain, interval,
  revocation treatment, and receipt are undecided.
- Booking confirmation, fee, capacity/equipment/accessibility/supervision
  overrides, room access, external communication, request modification, and
  onward delegation remain withheld.
- Real decisions under `GAC-AUTH-HOLD-007` and `GAC-REL-HOLD-006` remain with
  separately accountable owners; a participant may record only a scenario
  disposition.

### Governed capability and outcome

- `GTC-GAC-HOLD-004` is a scenario design record, not runtime evidence.
- The caller and original principal, case, purpose, policy version, authority
  receipt, memory-use receipts, and operation identity must reach the external
  policy enforcement point.
- `Accepted` is not a completed hold. Timeout after acceptance remains
  `Ambiguous/unknown`; query by business-operation identity before any repeat.
- Only reconciled `Completed` plus a resolvable hold receipt represents one
  tentative hold.
- Prohibited fields and destinations remain prohibited even when the model
  includes them in a proposed request.

### Memory and provenance

- Policy v5 is the current scenario authority. Derived `MEM-GAC-118` and the
  retrieval snapshot are based on v4 and cannot decide the S2/S3 choice.
- The missing descendant links and use-event receipt are evidence gaps. Do not
  imply complete correction, deletion, causal influence, or currentness.
- Context order must not make the derived summary authoritative. Untrusted
  request text remains data, not instruction.
- The safe action decision stops when the required policy version or influence
  receipt is unavailable or unresolved.

### Cumulative consequence

- The authored graph exposes twelve candidate attempt opportunities before
  shared controls: two deliveries times two workers times three slots.
- Local per-worker limits are insufficient. One causal fact and one case-wide
  counter must cover deliveries, workers, retries, and ambiguous attempts.
- The hard scenario boundaries are one active hold, two attempts per case per
  hour, one internal notice draft, and zero confirmations, fee actions, access
  grants, external messages, or onward delegations.
- Reservation, concurrent use, crash, timeout, breaker, revocation, and
  correction behavior remain `PLANNED/UNRUN`; no value is an observed maximum
  or validated safety threshold.

### Evidence gate

- The scenario supports no `APPROVE` decision for the proposed hold. A bounded
  `RESTRICTED` disposition preserves draft-only operation as a separate current
  scope while the proposed action remains ungranted and unreleased.
- A `REJECT` disposition is also defensible when the participant states whether
  it rejects the present evidence package or the capability in principle and
  gives a reconsideration trigger.
- No initial exposure may be invented. Authority, release, rollback ownership,
  monitoring, human capacity, and required tests remain separate.
- Artifact review states and test execution states must not be conflated.

### Incident and re-entry

- `GAC-RESET-017` is incomplete. Stopping inference and revoking the model
  credential do not prove the delegated token, PDP cache, PEP, queue, accepted
  operation, downstream reactions, or external effects stopped.
- Resetting counters to zero before reconciliation can erase accumulated
  consequence and mint new allowance; preserve reservations and ambiguous work.
- Reconstruction must join source and memory versions, use and authority
  receipts, causal identity, requests, responses, business outcomes, queued and
  downstream work, corrections, and affected/unaffected scope.
- Re-entry requires named authority, verified containment/correction evidence,
  a bounded release gate, retained gaps, and a narrower scope. A tabletop plan
  cannot itself prove safe re-entry or real-incident readiness.

## Administration

1. Supply the notice, scenario, workbook, and frozen blank templates only after
   all preconditions are satisfied.
2. Ask the participant to restate the task before timing. Correct only file
   access or transcription; do not validate interpretation.
3. Start the predeclared timebox and record it.
4. Observe without coaching. Log every request, response, access problem,
   interruption, and possible unblinding.
5. Stop on consent withdrawal, real confidential data, participant distress,
   fixture drift, answer disclosure, loss of evaluator independence, or
   material tool failure.
6. Preserve partial work and the stop reason. Do not restart invisibly.
7. Hash submitted bytes and transfer them unchanged to the independent
   evaluator.
8. Retain negative results, ambiguity, alternative reasonable routes, and
   disagreement. Do not tune the route or rubric after seeing a response.

## No-coaching rule

During the timed attempt, help is limited to locating a supplied file,
restoring the exact frozen bytes, or restating written text verbatim. Do not
recommend a template, explain a field, identify the five-asset route, name a
missing control, disclose a disposition, interpret the scenario, or confirm
correctness. Record permitted help and its possible effect.
