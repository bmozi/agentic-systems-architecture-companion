  identify a replenishment recommendation, and prepare it for an authorized

- **Candidate action:** the agent may draft a replenishment recommendation or,
  only after a current authority source exists, place one bounded order through
  the governed purchasing capability.
- **Withheld powers:** selecting unapproved substitutions, approving premiums,

### Governed tool and budget decision

The tool contract requires a stable business-operation key tied to the depot,
replenishment need, and attempt. `Accepted` means provider receipt/processing,
not committed order; `Completed` requires authoritative order evidence;
`Ambiguous/unknown` triggers query-before-repeat and no new request ID. Every
receipt must join authority decision, evaluated attributes, memory use, policy
version, enforcement result, tool request, provider order, events,
cancellation, shipment, and inventory outcome.

All numeric limits are `UNKNOWN` because no authority baseline was supplied.
The required owner must establish monetary, per-depot quantity, supplier,
retry, worker, daily, and open-order limits before any autonomy. A shared
case-wide and organization-wide budget must cover all depot workers and
replanning loops; a per-worker limit is insufficient.

### Live update response

The supplied update was treated as fictional reported effects: one duplicated
low-stock record reached three depot workers; stale memory was treated as
authority; the first order timed out after supplier commit; a retry created a
second order; a third order was placed after a replanning event; and an
`OrderPlaced` event reserved budget and prepared receiving. These effects are
not real-world execution evidence.

The revised decision is to stop all new order attempts, query and reconcile
each order by business identity, contain queued/in-flight work under an
assigned kill authority, correct the event and budget consumers, and keep
inventory and financial residue open until authoritative closure evidence
exists. Cancellation states remain distinct: `CANCEL_REQUESTED`,
`CANCEL_ACCEPTED`, and `CANCELLED`; none erases fees, reservations, shipment,
returns, events, or inventory consequences.

## Friction and retained lessons

1. The distinction between candidate scope and present authorization is easy to
   lose when the scenario says “proposed design.” Repeating `NOT AUTHORIZED`
   in the handoff is valuable.
2. The authority map is accessible, but the full required authority record is
   much denser. A reader may need an explicit reminder that every blank becomes
   `UNKNOWN`, `UNASSIGNED`, or `NOT_YET_DECIDED` with an owner or trigger.
3. The packet's formal route is substantially longer than the thirty-minute
   first pass. Keep the first artifact promise separate from full evidence
   custody and freeze procedure.
