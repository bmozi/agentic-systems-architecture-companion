**Packet:** AG-RV-PILOT-001 version 1.0.0

## Purpose

You are invited to use architecture materials against a fictional business
scenario. We are testing whether the materials are understandable and useful,
not testing your job performance, intelligence, or employer.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop at any time. The
result is not a certification, employment assessment, security review, or
production approval.

## Confidentiality boundary

Use only the fictional Cedar Lane scenario. Do not enter names, employers,
customers, real architecture, confidential incidents, credentials, personal
data, or identifiable security weaknesses. The facilitator will stop and
redirect if such information enters the session.

## Planned minimal record

- participant code and optional broad role/experience band;
- elapsed time and materials supplied;
- completed fictional-scenario artifacts;
- questions, pauses, revisions, and assistance;
- outside-team read-back and seeded-failure observations; and
- de-identified findings, including negative and stopped results.

Audio, video, screen recording, contact details, and employer identity are not
part of the default record. Separate explicit consent and retention approval
are required for any recording.
